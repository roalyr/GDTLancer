# TRUTH LORE CONSTRAINTS

**Version:** 2.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-24  
**Status:** Approved Lore Rules (Digital Board Game)

---

## Pillar 1: The Human Nucleus & No Institutions

The setting represents a raw, isolated frontier. There are no sprawling galactic corporations or sweeping empires. Society is localized to individual infrastructure nodes and tight-knit communities. Space is a hostile backdrop framing fragile human collectives.

#### What This Prohibits
- ❌ UI/Tags using terms like "corporation", "empire", or "military fleet".
- ❌ Treating stations as generic hubs. Each is a specific community board.

#### What This Requires
- ✅ Factions are small clans, families, or local alliances.
- ✅ Board interactions focus on personal grudges, favors, and survival.

---

## Pillar 2: The Weight of Travel & Mortality

Spaceflight is a non-trivial, perilous expedition demanding logistical sacrifice. Ships are not everyday cars. The persistent reality of physical vulnerability (radiation, structural fatigue, zero-g physiological decay, finite life support) anchors the lore.
- **Ownership:** Vessels are primarily community-owned (the default), though vessel-colonies and privately-owned ships exist as rare exceptions.
- **Scarce Command:** Pilots and captains are rare and highly consequential roles.

#### What This Prohibits
- ❌ Mechanics allowing trivial, consequence-free fast travel.
- ❌ Board states where every NPC is a pilot.

#### What This Requires
- ✅ Travel actions heavily tax resources and time (World Clock ticks).
- ✅ Vessel destruction or damage permanently mutates the board state and character availability.

---

## Pillar 3: Community-Centric (and Loner) Progression

Progression strips away linear gear-score grinding, focusing instead on community health, interpersonal dependencies, and resource cache management. Exploration motivation must be grounded in relational necessity, not personal glory accumulation or abstract stat hoarding.
- **Viability:** While community is integral, **loner roleplay must be viable** for players who choose isolation.

#### What This Prohibits
- ❌ Loot-drop gear scaling (e.g., "Level 5 Laser").
- ❌ Victory states tied solely to personal wealth accumulation or abstract glory.

#### What This Requires
- ✅ Board states visually reflect the health and stability of the sector via visual tags and tokens.
- ✅ Progression is tied to manipulating board tags and tokens, acquiring impactful Asset Cards, and navigating the World Clock.

---

## Pillar 4: Paradigm Shifts & The Anomalous

Establish strict, grounded rules of physics, resource scarcity, and industrial technology first. The lore must be coherent enough that anomalous margin events register as genuinely dissonant.
- **Rule:** Rare, rule-breaking phenomena exist on the outer margins. They must shatter assumptions and are explicitly prohibited from being pre-explained or hand-waved with pseudo-science.

---

## Pillar 5: Layered Meta-Worldbuilding

The lore history and cosmic realities are stratified and must not be front-loaded.
- **Tier 1:** Immediate survival narrative (The community board).
- **Tier 2:** Deeper systemic truths and political forces (Revealed through mid-game exploration).
- **Tier 3:** Forgotten historical layers and cosmic realities (Outer margins).

---

## Pillar 6: Transhumanism (The Cost of Endurance)

Modifications exist on a spectrum and carry meaningful narrative and mechanical cost. The lore must neither romanticize augmentation as a simple power-up nor condemn it—the ambiguity is the point.
- **Rule:** Asset Cards representing biological or mechanical modifications must carry trade-off tags, reflecting what is surrendered when human beings rebuild themselves for the void.

---

## Pillar 7: Transcendent Resonance

A strict protection clause for inexplicable mysticism within the hard sci-fi framework.
- **Rule:** The game must reserve space for deeply emotional, inexplicable human connections (e.g., simultaneous awareness across vacuum, lingering presences in derelicts).
- **Prohibition:** These occurrences must **never** be given in-game explanations, scientific jargon, or clarifying dialogue. They remain unanswerable enigmas.
